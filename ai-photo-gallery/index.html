What makes Filtored different from a normal gallery app?

A normal gallery mostly shows photos by date. Filtored is an AI photo organizer: it helps group people, surface events, find duplicate photos, and make the camera roll searchable without needing an account for core features.

Who is this AI gallery for?

Filtored is built for people with thousands of photos on their Android phone who want a faster way to clean, search, and organize their gallery.

  • People who want to remove duplicate photos.
  • People who want photos grouped by faces, events, and moments.
  • People who want private, on-device photo organization.
  • People who want a smart gallery without creating another account.

Is Filtored an offline AI photo organizer?

Yes. Filtored is designed around on-device AI. That means the main photo organization features work on your phone, and your photos do not need to be uploaded for normal use.
