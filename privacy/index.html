Privacy Policy for Filtored AI Gallery

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Filtored helps you organize photos and videos with a privacy-first approach. Media analysis is performed on your device. This policy explains what data is used, where it is processed, and what controls you have.

What Data the App Uses

  • Photos and videos in your library that you allow the app to access
  • Derived metadata generated by the app (for example: tags, grouping, duplicate/similar clusters, favorites, hidden/trash state, and scan versions)
  • Basic technical diagnostics needed to keep features working (for example, scan progress and error logs stored on-device)

The app does not require account sign-in for core functionality and does not collect contacts, messages, or precise location data.

How Processing Works

  • On-device processing: Scanning, tagging, grouping, duplicate/similar detection, and face grouping/photo organization run locally on your device.
  • No internet required for analysis: Core media analysis features do not require an internet connection.

Storage and Retention

  • On your device: The app stores its metadata locally so features like search, organize, hidden, and trash work quickly.
  • Trash: Items you remove via organize flows are moved to trash and can be restored until permanently deleted.
  • Permanent deletion: Permanently deleted items are removed from device storage and are not recoverable through the app.
  • No cloud copy for analysis: Your media is not uploaded to cloud services for analysis in this version.

Sharing

  • By default, your media is not shared with third parties.
  • Filtored does not sell personal data.
  • The app does not provide public sharing or user-to-user media exchange features.

Permissions and Controls

  • Media permission: Required to read/organize photos and videos.
  • Notifications (device/platform dependent): May be used to show progress while long scans run.
  • Hidden/Safe: Sensitive items can be placed in hidden storage protected by device authentication where supported by the operating system.
  • Reset controls: You can clear tags/rescan at any time in Settings.

Advertising and Analytics

In this version, Filtored does not use third-party advertising SDKs.

Security

Because media analysis is on-device in this version, your photos and videos stay on your phone during analysis. For additional protection, keep your device up to date and use screen lock or biometrics.

Children's Privacy

Filtored is intended for a general audience and is not designed for children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as features evolve. The "Last updated" date will reflect the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information on the app's store listing.
