Patch Notes

Here you'll find what's new, improved, and fixed in recent updates of Filtored.

Version 2.0.0

Latest
October 10, 2026

New

  • Contextual Search - describe what you're looking for in your own words, like "kids at the beach" or "birthday cake".
  • Manage faces - spot faces that don't belong in a profile and move them to the right person, or a new one, in a tap. Remove multiple faces at once.
  • Mixed profile alerts - Filtored lets you know when a profile might include more than one person, and keeps the reminder in your notifications until you get to it.
  • Event search & saved events - find any event quickly and keep your favorites one tap away, now with holiday artwork.

Enhanced

  • Built for huge libraries - ultra-high count galleries now load, scroll, search and organize much faster.
  • Smoother scanning - the app stays fast and responsive while it organizes your library in the background.
  • Smarter scanning - scanning adjusts to your phone's capabilities and steps aside while you're using the app.
  • More accurate people - better grouping of the same person, fewer mix-ups between lookalikes, and better handling of kids and babies.
  • Tidier notifications - people suggestions are split into Merging and Managing, and show one at a time so they don't get in your way.
  • On This Day - your best memories are now separated from the clutter.
  • Similar photos are now Burst photos - clearer name, same quick cleanup.

Fixed

  • Fixed a bug where the app could freeze when opening Cleanup during a scan.
  • Fixed a bug where new photos weren't organized while the app was closed on Android.
  • Fixed a bug where some faces could be saved incorrectly and mix up people. Affected photos are fixed automatically.
  • Fixed a bug where opening a person's profile could fail.
  • Fixed a bug where renaming someone from the photo viewer showed an error.
  • Fixed memory issues during face scanning that could slow the app down over time.

Version 1.2.3

October 2, 2026

Fixed

  • Fixed a bug where photos re-sorted themselves for a few seconds every time the gallery opened.
  • Fixed a bug where photos copied from another device all appeared under today's date.
  • Fixed a bug where pull-to-refresh reloaded the whole library instead of just what changed.
  • Fixed a bug where the people row redrew the whole screen and made the app stutter while idle.
  • Fixed a bug where merging people could freeze the screen for several seconds.
  • Fixed a bug where the cleanup hub stuttered while scrolling.
  • Fixed a bug where sharing did nothing on iPad.
  • Fixed a bug where Share diagnostics came back empty on iPhone.
  • Fixed a bug where the edit pencil on face circles was cut off.

Version 1.2.2

September 30, 2026

New

  • Filtored is starting testing on iPhone and iPad.

Enhanced

  • Finding similar photos is much faster.
  • Similar-photos screen redesign.
  • Photos with text are read instantly.

Fixed

  • Fixed a bug where labels and category chips showed nothing on iPhone.
  • Fixed a bug where text saved from a photo could be wiped by the background scan.
  • Fixed a bug where sharing a photo stored in iCloud came back empty.
  • Fixed a bug where deleting a photo from full screen left it in the similar-photos grid.
  • Fixed memory management issues that could cause slowness and crashes.

Version 1.2.1

September 28, 2026

New

  • On This Day - see the photos you took on this date every year Day or Week view.
  • My Family - suggests you photos for deletion that were received from chat groups that do not include your named profiles.

Enhanced

  • Smoother people row - face pictures in the gallery now show up in steady batches instead of the screen redrawing every few faces.
  • Profiles open anywhere - tapping a person inside the cleanup screens now opens their full profile.

Fixed

  • Battery drain while idle - fixed the gallery quietly redrawing itself over and over on libraries with lots of people.
  • Stutter while scanning - fixed short freezes caused by the app writing too many background log lines while merging profiles.
  • Leftover loading - fixed photo previews that kept loading after you closed their sheet.

Version 1.2.0

September 27, 2026

New

  • Read text in photos - select any text directly from an image to copy, share, or search it online.
  • QR & barcode scanning in the photo viewer - links open with one tap.
  • Search by words inside photos, not just tags.
  • Your own labels - add custom labels to any photo or edit labels found by AI.
  • Similar Photos - find near-identical shots and clear them out.
  • Event albums rework - tag events with existing or custom tags.
  • Notifications system in the profile tab.

Enhanced

  • Faster people merging - combine similar faces much quicker and pick multiple matches at once.
  • Smooth swipe navigation - slide search and filters open naturally with your finger.
  • Drag-to-select - drag across the Events grid to select multiple photos in one go.
  • Independent filters - use category filters (People, Food, Documents) without resetting search results.
  • Live cleanup progress - see real-time progress while bulk deleting photos.
  • Instant Cleanup hub - opens right away and remembers scan counts so you don't wait.
  • Languages - Event tags and holidays are now available in Hebrew and Russian.

Fixed

  • Accidental face merges - fixed a bug where two people in one photo were grouped into one profile.
  • Grey avatars - fixed profiles getting stuck on a blank silhouette instead of showing face photos.
  • Names on profile splits - fixed names getting lost when separating mistakenly combined people.
  • Missing event albums - fixed newly tagged events occasionally losing their album.
  • Duplicate accuracy - tightened duplicate detection to stop grouping non-identical photos.
  • Scan freezing - fixed scans getting stuck mid-way and made the time remaining estimate accurate.
  • Screen layout issues - fixed text cut-offs and broken screens when using large system fonts.
  • Limited photo access - fixed new photos not being scanned when using limited Android gallery permissions.
