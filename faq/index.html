Questions, answered

The short version of everything people ask us.

Is Filtored free?

Yes. Filtored is free to download on Google Play.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. All the AI runs on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server — the app works exactly the same in airplane mode.

Do I need an account?

No. Install, scan, done. There is nothing to sign up for.

Which phones does it work on?

Any Android phone running Android 5.0 or newer, with about 100MB of free space.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — Filtored is Android-first. An iPhone version is something we'd love to do later.

Can it delete photos without asking me?

Never. Filtored only suggests duplicates and junk shots — nothing is removed until you review and confirm it yourself.

Found a bug, or have an idea?

We want to hear it — reach us through the contact page.
