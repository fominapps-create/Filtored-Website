Frequently Asked Questions

What is Filtored?

Filtored is an AI-powered smart photo organization tool designed to automatically clean, organize, and tag your image galleries. It helps users clear up device storage by safely identifying and removing duplicates, while also grouping similar images together.

How does Filtored work?
  • Step 1 - ScanFiltored scans your gallery with on-device AI.
  • Step 2 - OrganizeYour media is organized into categories, objects, and faces.
  • Step 3 - FilterReview suggestions and decide what to keep or remove.
What type of photos can Filtored identify?

Filtored can identify photos with: people, animals, food, scenery, documents, objects, faces, text, barcodes and QR codes.

Is Filtored free?

Yes. Filtored is free to download on Google Play. It includes in-app purchases to unlock extra features.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Unlike some native and other galleries, Filtored organizes your photos right on your phone, without sending them to the cloud, keeping your photos private and under your control.

How is my privacy handled?

Filtored is offline by design: your photos never leave your phone and all AI processing happens locally. There are no uploads and no account needed, so your photos stay private. Read our Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account?

No. Install, scan, done. There is nothing to sign up for.

What permissions does Filtored need?
  • Media access - to view your photos and videos.
  • Notification permission - for background scanning.
Which devices are supported?

Any Android phone or tablet running Android 7.0 or newer, with 100MB of free space.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet - Filtored is Android-first. An iPhone version is something we'd love to do later.

Can it delete photos without asking me?

Never. Filtored uses smart analysis to suggest photos for deletion, but nothing is ever removed automatically. You review and confirm every deletion yourself.
