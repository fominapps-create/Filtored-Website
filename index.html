FAQ

know.

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

All questions
Is Filtored free?

Yes. Filtored is free on Google Play, with optional Plus and Premium plans for extra features. There are no ads in any version.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Filtored sorts your photos right on your phone, without sending them to the cloud.

Do I need an account?

No. Install it, open it, and your gallery gets sorted. There is nothing to sign up for.

Can it delete photos without asking me?

Never. Cleanup suggestions are gathered for you, but you review and confirm every deletion. Deleted photos stay in the trash for 30 days.

Which devices are supported?

Any Android phone or tablet running Android 7.0 or newer. An iPhone version is something we'd love to do later.
