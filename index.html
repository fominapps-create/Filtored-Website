Filtored is built with privacy in mind, minimizing unnecessary data collection. Photos aren't shared with third parties by default. Local, account-free processing without relying on external services means your gallery stays under your control.

  • No Cloud

    Your photos aren't uploaded to the cloud for processing by default.

  • On-device AI

    AI processing happens directly on your device.

  • No Account

    Filtored features don't require an account or online profile.

  • Works Offline

    Core functionality doesn't depend on an internet connection.
