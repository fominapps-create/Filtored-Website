Best Gallery & Photo Cleanup Apps for Android

September 8, 2026

Your Android gallery has to handle more than just displaying photos. Every day, new photos, screenshots, videos, and downloads pile up — until your library turns into thousands of files that are hard to search, organize, or clean up. Different apps take different approaches: some focus on cloud backup, others on local organization, and some are dedicated purely to finding duplicates. Here's how the most useful ones compare.

Filtored

Focus: AI-powered gallery, organization, privacy, and cleanup  ·  Pricing: Free plan, with Plus and Premium subscriptions for extra features and limits

Filtored is an AI-powered gallery replacement built to manage both the photos you take today and the years of photos you've already accumulated. Strengths

  • Automatic Events instead of manual grouping

Google Photos

Focus: Cloud backup, search, and AI photo management  ·  Pricing: Free with 15GB shared Google Account storage; Google One plans add more

One of the most comprehensive photo apps on Android, combining cloud backup, search, organization, editing, and sharing. It automatically groups similar photos into stacks and offers storage-management tools.

Strengths

  • 15GB of free cloud storage
  • Excellent backup and cross-device access
  • Powerful AI search, recognition, and editing tools
  • Strong sharing and ecosystem integration

Limitations

  • Free storage is shared across Photos, Gmail, and Drive
  • Larger libraries need a paid Google One plan
  • Cleanup is a side feature, not the main focus

Best for: People who want cloud backup, search, and cross-device access above all else.

Find Google Photos on Google Play

Aves Gallery

Focus: Advanced local gallery and metadata management  ·  Pricing: Free and open-source

A powerful local gallery and media explorer built around navigation, search, metadata, tags, and a wide range of image and video formats, from common JPEG/MP4 to SVG, multi-page TIFF, and 360° media.

Strengths

  • Free, open-source, no subscription
  • Excellent metadata support and format coverage
  • Advanced search, tags, albums, and maps

Limitations

  • Feels more technical than mainstream galleries
  • Not built around automated cleanup
  • No cloud-backup ecosystem

Best for: Power users who want detailed, free control over a large local photo collection.

Find Aves Gallery on Google Play

F-Stop Gallery

Focus: Advanced manual photo organization  ·  Pricing: Free version; Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription)

Focused on giving users control over how their library is organized: tags, ratings, smart albums, nested folders, metadata, and custom sorting.

Strengths

  • Advanced tagging and ratings, smart albums
  • Nested folders, EXIF/XMP/IPTC metadata support
  • Private photo vault, custom themes
  • One-time Pro purchase instead of a subscription

Limitations

  • More manual than AI-first gallery apps
  • Some features require paying for Pro
  • Cleanup and duplicate detection aren't the focus

Best for: People who want detailed manual control without a recurring subscription.

Find F-Stop Gallery on Google Play

Piktures

Focus: Local, cloud, and network photo management  ·  Pricing: Free basic version; Premium/Prime tiers add cloud features (varies by region)

An all-in-one gallery that brings together media from your phone, SD cards, USB drives, cloud services, and network storage, including a private vault, editing tools, and search.

Strengths

  • Local and cloud media in one gallery
  • Google Drive, OneDrive, and network storage support
  • Private vault and photo/video editing

Limitations

  • More complex than a basic gallery
  • Contains ads and in-app purchases
  • Less focused on automated AI organization

Best for: People who want one gallery to manage local media alongside cloud and network storage.

Find Piktures on Google Play

Fossify Gallery

Focus: Simple, private, and customizable local gallery  ·  Pricing: Free, no subscription

Takes the opposite approach to cloud-heavy platforms: an open-source gallery built around local media, privacy, and simplicity, with no ads and no unnecessary permissions.

Strengths

  • Free, open-source, no ads, no subscription
  • Recycle bin, PIN/pattern/fingerprint protection
  • EXIF metadata removal, extensive customization

Limitations

  • More traditional gallery experience
  • No large AI-powered organization system
  • Limited automated cleanup

Best for: People who want a straightforward, private gallery without ads or subscriptions.

Find Fossify Gallery on Google Play

Amazon Photos

Focus: Cloud photo storage and automatic backup  ·  Pricing: Free with 5GB; Prime members in supported countries get unlimited full-resolution photo storage

Primarily a cloud storage and backup service that automatically backs up photos and videos, with search, albums, sharing, and image recognition across phones, tablets, and computers.

Strengths

  • 5GB free storage; strong value for Prime members
  • Automatic cloud backup at full resolution
  • Search, recognition, Memories and Stories

Limitations

  • Primarily cloud-storage focused
  • Prime benefits vary by country
  • Limited dedicated cleanup tools

Best for: Amazon and Prime users who mainly want cloud backup and storage.

Find Amazon Photos on Google Play

Remo Duplicate Photos Remover

Focus: Dedicated duplicate-photo cleanup  ·  Pricing: Free

Unlike the other apps here, Remo isn't trying to replace your gallery — it's a dedicated cleanup tool that scans your phone or MicroSD card for duplicate and similar images, groups them for review, and lets you delete unwanted copies while keeping an original.

Strengths

  • Free, no subscription required
  • Finds exact and similar images
  • Scans internal and MicroSD storage

Limitations

  • Not a gallery replacement
  • Only covers duplicates, not screenshots or large videos
  • Requires a separate app alongside your normal gallery

Best for: People who like their existing gallery but need a dedicated duplicate-finder.

Find Remo on Google Play

Gallery apps vs. photo cleanup apps

Looking at pricing models reveals an important difference between these apps. Some are free forever, like Aves and Fossify. Others use a one-time purchase, like F-Stop Pro. Google Photos and Amazon Photos are tied mainly to cloud-storage subscriptions or memberships, while Piktures uses multiple paid tiers for people who need more cloud functionality. Dedicated cleanup apps like Remo can be completely free, but they solve a much narrower problem: finding duplicates.

Filtored sits somewhere different — a full gallery replacement that also brings cleanup, recognition, privacy, and organization into the same experience, so you don't need one app to browse your photos, another to find duplicates, another to hide private photos, and another to organize your library.

Which one is right for you?

Google Photos is a strong choice if cloud backup, search, and cross-device access are your priorities. Amazon Photos makes sense if you're already invested in Amazon and want cloud storage, particularly with Prime. Aves Gallery is a strong option for power users who want an advanced, free local gallery. F-Stop Gallery suits people who want detailed manual control and are comfortable paying once for Pro. Piktures is useful if you want to combine local storage with cloud drives and network storage. Fossify Gallery is a good choice if privacy, simplicity, and no subscription matter more than AI-powered organization. Remo is useful when your main problem is duplicate photos and you don't need a new gallery.

Filtored takes a broader approach: an AI-powered gallery that combines everyday organization, recognition, cleanup, privacy, security, and customization in one app, with all AI processing happening on your device.
